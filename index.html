mc_server — Personal Minecraft Backups

mc_server is a private desktop/server utility operated by its owner to back up a Minecraft world and related server configuration to the owner's Google Drive using rclone. It is not a public hosting service and does not offer accounts to other users.

How backups are handled

Backups are created locally, uploaded to a configured Google Drive folder, and checked for integrity. The utility maintains a limited history of backup archives.

The application's Google Drive authorization is broader than the configured backup folder. The folder restriction is enforced by the utility's configuration, not by the OAuth permission itself.

This website provides information about the utility. It does not provide access to backup archives or collect Google credentials.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy policy for details about Google Drive access, storage, retention, and revoking authorization.

Contact the operator through GitHub Issues. Issues are public; please do not include credentials, backup files, or private player information.
